SEVERITY SCALE

HOW RESULTS ARE GRADED

Every scan resolves to one of three tiers, so the next step is never ambiguous.

CLEAR

Minor or cosmetic. Standard care advice is shown — no escalation needed.

MODERATE

Warrants monitoring and basic first aid. Logging the incident is recommended.

CRITICAL

Needs prompt attention — nearby hospital lookup and parent notification are surfaced immediately.

YOUR DATA, PROTECTED

DATA PRIVACY NOTICE

SkinGuard stores scanned images and AI findings in a secure database. This is done in compliance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), and your consent is required before every scan.

01

What We Collect

The photo captured or uploaded during a scan, together with the AI's assessment findings.

02

Why We Collect It

To generate the assessment, keep a clinical record for follow‑up, and to help train and improve the AI model.

03

Your Consent

Before each scan you are asked to explicitly agree. You may decline and choose not to proceed.

04

Access Control

Stored records are only accessible to authorized clinic staff through the admin records system.

05

Your Rights

Under RA 10173, you have the right to be informed, to access, and to request correction or deletion of your data.
